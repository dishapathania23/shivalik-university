About Shivalik University

A NAAC Grade A++ accredited university dedicated to research, eco-green sustainability, and global leadership in higher education.

OUR HERITAGE & VISION

Empowering Students in the Lap of Nature

Shivalik University (SU) was established with a vision to build a world-class center of higher learning nestled in the serene, eco-sustainable pine hills of Shimla.

Our 200+ acre solar-powered campus combines cutting-edge Industry 4.0 laboratories with a peaceful academic atmosphere, enabling students to innovate, research, and excel.
