School of Law

BA LL.B (Hons.) Integrated 5-Year Law

Duration: 5 Years
Annual Fee: INR 1,50,000 / year
Degree: Full-Time Campus Degree

Program Overview

The BA LL.B (Hons.) Integrated 5-Year Law at Shivalik University is designed to deliver rigorous academic training combined with hands-on practical exposure. Taught by distinguished faculty and industry experts in Shimla, students gain practical mastery over theoretical foundations and industry 4.0 applications.

Eligibility & Admission Criteria

Academic Qualification: 10+2 in any stream (Min 45%)
Entrance Exam: SU-SAT 2026 (Shivalik University Scholastic Aptitude Test) or valid Qualifying Exam Score.

Career & Placement Opportunities

Graduates of BA LL.B (Hons.) Integrated 5-Year Law are placed in top corporate and research roles including:

Corporate Legal Counsel Advocate Legal Researcher Arbitrator
